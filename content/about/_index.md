## Hello! I'm Rogelio

I'm a **Senior Software Engineer** who genuinely loves turning complex business problems into elegant technical solutions. With over 9 years in the industry, I've had the privilege of building systems that process millions of transactions for companies like Mastercard and Thomson Reuters.

## My Journey

My path into software engineering started in Durango, Mexico, where I first discovered the magic of making computers do exactly what I wanted them to do. That early fascination turned into a degree in Computer Systems Engineering from Instituto Tecnologico de Durango, where I graduated with honors while already taking on freelance projects.

What I didn't expect was how much I'd come to love the *messy* parts of software development. Legacy migrations? I find them fascinating - there's something deeply satisfying about taking a decade-old codebase and breathing new life into it. Performance optimization? Give me a slow query and an afternoon, and I'm in my element.

My career has taken me from building document management systems to modernizing financial platforms to leading teams on enterprise-scale projects. Each role taught me something new: Thomson Reuters showed me the importance of reliability at scale. Mastercard taught me that in fintech, there's no such thing as "good enough" when it comes to security and accuracy. EPAM gave me the opportunity to lead and mentor while tackling increasingly complex challenges.

## What Drives Me

I'm not the engineer who chases every new framework. Instead, I focus on **choosing the right tool for the job** and using it exceptionally well. That said, I stay curious - I'm currently exploring Kotlin more deeply and keeping an eye on how AI is reshaping our industry.

What really gets me excited is taking a system from "it works" to "it works *beautifully*." That might mean:

- Reducing a 4-hour deployment to 30 minutes through containerization
- Turning a confusing codebase into something a new team member can understand in days, not weeks
- Finding the bottleneck that's been frustrating users and eliminating it

## Beyond the Code

When I'm not writing code, you'll find me exploring new technologies, contributing to open-source projects, and mentoring developers who are earlier in their journey. I believe strongly in giving back to the community that helped me grow.

I'm also a firm believer in work-life balance and continuous learning. The best engineers I know are curious about the world beyond their IDE, and I try to bring that same curiosity to everything I do.
